P2 · Story + products at risk

The same sentence and action, then only the products at risk, one line each in their own unit. Healthy products are collapsed.

Pros

  • Every problem at this site at a glance
  • Stays short when few are at risk
  • Answers "what's wrong here?" fully

Cons

  • Many at-risk products = dense again
  • A bit more to read before the action
  • No timing

P3 · Story + a small chart

Adds a 7-day bar chart for the driving product, so you see when it goes wrong. It has a table alternative. The most information of the three, but still one product at a time.

Pros

  • Shows when it goes wrong
  • Quick for visual readers; has a table version

Cons

  • Most to take in; charts need reading
  • Only the main product gets a chart
  • Cramped in a sidebar or phone

Recommended: P2, with tap-to-expand: tapping a product line opens its 7-day mini chart from P3.
In all three, "See what to do →" opens the suggested transfers for this site. Quantities stay in each product's own unit and are never added across products. The full per-product projection table moves behind "Site details".
