Supply routes on the map

The lines from the distribution center to its stores. How much of them should you see?

⏸ Pause link animation

R1 · Always on (today)

Every route drawn, gently animated, with a pause button.

Pros

  • The network shape is obvious at once
  • Already built and tested

Cons

  • With ~100 sites it becomes a web of lines, the density you want to avoid
  • Movement pulls the eye even when nothing is wrong
  • Lines cross the markers you're trying to read

R2 · Only for the site you picked

No lines until you pick a site; then its own route(s) appear, with distance and travel time.

Pros

  • Calm map: just markers until you ask
  • The line you see is the one that matters to your decision
  • Distance and days right on the map

Cons

  • You don't see the overall network shape at first
  • The supply-flow work from W5 is mostly retired
Layers
☐ All supply routes
☑ Selected site's routes

R3 · R2 plus a "Layers" switch

Like Google Maps' layers button: by default you only see the picked site's routes; "All supply routes" draws the whole network, static, when you want it.

Pros

  • Calm by default, full picture on request
  • Familiar from maps apps
  • No always-moving lines

Cons

  • One more control on screen
  • Some users never discover the layer
  • Slightly more to build than R2

Recommended: R3. Calm by default, and the Google Maps "Layers" pattern you asked for gives the full network when you want it.
