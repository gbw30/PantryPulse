How much should the background map show?

Bundled with the app (no online map service, no key, nothing leaves the machine). Markers are identical in all three.

B1 · Land outline only

The state/coast shape and the markers. Nothing else.

Pros

  • Calmest; markers are the only thing to look at
  • Smallest file, simplest to build

Cons

  • Hard to tell where you are once zoomed in
  • No towns to orient by

B2 · + county lines and a few city names

Faint county borders and major city names that appear as you zoom.

Pros

  • Easy to orient: "the stores east of Orlando"
  • Still calm; labels are faint and few
  • Public-domain data, works offline

Cons

  • A bundled data file per US region (a few MB)
  • City labels can collide with markers; needs care

B3 · + major highways

Adds interstates and main highways, like a road map.

Pros

  • Most familiar, most like Google Maps
  • Routes "make sense" next to real roads

Cons

  • Busiest; roads compete with markers and routes
  • Larger bundled data, more build work
  • Our routes are straight-line or Google estimates, not road paths, so they won't follow the roads drawn

Recommended: B2. Enough to know where you are, without competing with the danger markers.
