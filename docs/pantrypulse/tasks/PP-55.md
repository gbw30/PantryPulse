**Data and controls (only what the contract validates).**
- ① *Context:* planning-ready forecast from `planning-catalog.forecasts` (presenter may `Generate forecast` exactly as `/optimization` does, via the foundation helper); assumptions version (select; read-only summary of policy version); planning window 1/2/3 days (`planning_days`); scope is fixed: all 6 beneficiaries × 2 products, shown, not editable.
- ② *Transfer mode:* `HUB_ONLY` ("Hub to pantry only") / `HUB_AND_PANTRY` ("Hub and pantry-to-pantry"), **no default**; step stays `Incomplete` until chosen. Changing it after freezing creates a new scenario.
- ③ *Disruptions:* per beneficiary × product `DemandModifier`: multiplier 0–3, ≤4 decimals (default 1 = no change, shown as "baseline"); mode `CENTRAL`, or `CONSERVATIVE` with buffer fraction 0–1 only when allowed (Q-55-4). Client validation mirrors `or_inputs.py:455–475`; server 422 maps back to the field. No budget, distance, region or goal-weight sliders.
- ④ *Freeze & compare:* draft diff vs the parent scenario (or "new scenario"); `Create frozen scenario` → `POST scenarios` (`expected_source_revision` from the catalog; 409 → "Sources changed; refresh and freeze again"); then `Compare methods` → `POST scenarios/{id}/compare-methods` with `expected_scenario_hash` (= `payload.operational_hash`) and search seconds (1–300, default from methodology). 202 is "Queued", never a result.
- *Durable job:* `?job=<id>` in the URL; poll `GET jobs/{id}` ~2.5s; states QUEUED, RUNNING, STOPPING, SUCCEEDED, FAILED, TIMED_OUT, INTERRUPTED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED with `reason_code`; Cancel while running, Retry when terminal-non-success; refresh resumes; previous result stays visible with its timestamp; no percentage. On SUCCEEDED → `GET comparisons/{job_id}` + `GET scenarios/{scenario_id}`.
- *Results (per method; VERIFIED only, otherwise the method card shows its outcome/error code):* service coverage per product Σfulfilled/Σtarget; unmet units per product; AP / RP flagged goals (count of `ap`/`rp`, with group sizes); simulated cost points (`cost_minor`) and projected expired units (`waste_by_product`) per product. Baseline from `baseline_metrics`. Never households, food value in dollars, or an "equity score".
- *Projected Fill Rate by Site:* `service_ratio` per beneficiary for the selected product, both methods; `N/A` for null (zero target).
- *Key Trade-offs:* deterministic from metrics: largest same-product service gain and loss per site between methods (both directions), residual unmet, AP/RP count change, cost/expired difference. Phrase as "on the same frozen inputs"; no causal or universal claim; link each bullet to its R8 row.
- Visitor: Q-55-3. Nothing from the mockup (S-014, 86%, 2.6M, $1.1M, site names, Equity Score) appears.

**States.** Loading; visitor; no planning-ready forecast (step ① explains, presenter can generate); forecast job running/failed/not planning ready; validation failure per field; freeze 409 stale sources; compare 409 `SCENARIO_REVISION_MISMATCH`; job queued/running/stopping; failed/timed-out/interrupted/cancelled/expired with Retry; `PARTIAL` (one method failed — show it separately, never substitute); `FAILED`; native `FEASIBLE` ("feasible, not proven optimal"); `UNKNOWN`/`MODEL_INVALID` (no allocation shown); verified with zero transfers; stale/historical saved comparison; 503 worker/backend unavailable; network failure with Reconnect.

**Accessibility and motion.** Order: header → wizard steps in order (each a fieldset with legend "Step N of 4 · …") → submit buttons → job status (`role="status"`, announces terminal state once) → results → rail → trade-offs → details. Invalid input gets `aria-invalid` + message and keeps focus. Duplicate submits blocked while busy. Charts have tables. Mobile: one step visible, step indicator announces "Step 2 of 4".

**Acceptance.** `scenario-lab.spec.ts` (three widths, reduced motion, screenshots) asserts: no transfer-mode radio checked initially and Freeze disabled until chosen; multiplier >3 or 5 decimals blocks Freeze with a field message; freeze then compare POST the documented bodies once (double-click guarded); job states QUEUED→RUNNING→SUCCEEDED render and survive reload via `?job=`; FAILED shows Retry and keeps the previous result; PARTIAL shows one method's error and the other's results; fill-rate chart has 6 beneficiaries and `N/A` for zero target; no "household", "$", or "equity score" text; visitor sees saved-results state without enabled controls; `expectNoSeriousA11yViolations` per width and per step. `PP_RUN_CONNECTED=1` test: freeze → compare → verified results end to end.
